Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant with a blue-green color created by methane in its upper atmosphere. Unlike most planets, it rotates almost on its side, likely after a massive collision long ago.

Its faint rings and many moons travel around a world with some of the coldest planetary temperatures measured in the solar system. Uranus takes more than eighty Earth years to complete one orbit.
