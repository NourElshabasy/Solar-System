Neptune

Neptune is the most distant major planet and an ice giant with a deep blue atmosphere. Methane helps create its color, while high-altitude clouds drift above powerful storms.

Despite receiving very little sunlight, Neptune has some of the fastest winds in the solar system. Its largest moon, Triton, travels backward compared with the direction of Neptune's rotation.
