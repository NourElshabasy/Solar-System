Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest world to the Sun. Its rocky surface is covered with craters, giving it an appearance similar to our Moon.

With almost no atmosphere to hold heat, Mercury has some of the most extreme temperature changes in the solar system. A day on Mercury lasts longer than its year.
