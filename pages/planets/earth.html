Earth

Earth is our home planet and the only world currently known to support life. Liquid water covers most of its surface, while a protective atmosphere helps keep the climate suitable for living things.

Earth has active geology, shifting continents, deep oceans, and a magnetic field that shields the surface from much of the solar wind. Its single Moon also influences the planet's tides.
