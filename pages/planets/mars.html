Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet because iron minerals in its dusty surface have oxidized, or rusted. It has towering volcanoes, deep canyons, polar ice caps, and evidence of ancient flowing water.

Today Mars is cold and dry, but robotic explorers continue to study its rocks and atmosphere for clues about its past. Its two small moons, Phobos and Deimos, may be captured asteroids.
