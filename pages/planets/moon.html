Moon

The Moon is Earth's natural satellite and the fifth-largest moon in the solar system. Its surface is covered with craters, mountains, and broad plains formed by ancient volcanic activity.

The Moon has no substantial atmosphere or liquid water on its surface. Its gravity drives Earth's ocean tides, and its phases change as it travels around Earth.
