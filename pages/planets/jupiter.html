Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system, a giant world made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Its striped clouds are organized into powerful bands of storms.

The Great Red Spot is a long-lived storm larger than Earth. Jupiter also acts as a powerful gravitational anchor, guiding many comets and asteroids through the outer solar system.
