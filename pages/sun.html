The Sun

The Sun is the star at the center of our solar system and the source of nearly all the energy that reaches Earth. Its gravity holds the planets, moons, asteroids, and comets in their paths.

Deep inside the Sun, hydrogen atoms fuse together under immense heat and pressure. This nuclear fusion produces the light and warmth that power Earth's climate and make life possible.

Diameter1.39 million km
Age4.6 billion years
Surface temperature5,500 C
CompositionHydrogen and helium
